# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, harassment-free community around QuietLoad Report Analyzer.

* Be respectful and constructive; criticise ideas, not people.
* No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
* Protect privacy: do not post other people's or your employer's data, and never post unredacted database reports.
* Do not post copyrighted third-party material (reports, Oracle documentation text) without the right to do so.
* Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block contributors who break these rules.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub: use the private vulnerability reporting form (https://github.com/hannawissam/quietload-community-edition/security/advisories/new) and mark the report as a conduct issue. This is a short code; the owner may adopt
the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org) in full.
